What the assessment covers

The survey includes 25 questions across five structural areas of your sales organization. Your responses will help assess the current state of your revenue system and identify key themes and priorities that will serve as the foundation for a more focused and productive discussion.

01
Go-to-Market Strategy
How clearly your organization defines its target market, ideal customer profile, territory design, and growth strategy alignment.
02
Management and Coaching
The quality and consistency of your frontline management, coaching infrastructure, onboarding effectiveness, and leadership alignment.
03
Revenue Visibility
How clearly your leadership team can see pipeline quality, forecast accuracy, CRM data integrity, and real-time performance indicators.
04
Talent Motivation
Whether your compensation design, goal clarity, career architecture, and performance culture drive sustained discretionary effort.
05
Operational Controls and Governance
The rigor of your sales process enforcement, pipeline review discipline, forecast governance, and performance management cadence.
